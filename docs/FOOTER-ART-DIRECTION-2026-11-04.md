# Multisport footer artwork

Created with the built-in image-generation tool from the original footer illustration. The final artwork uses a continuous community park scene in the established forest-green, sage and lime palette. Website text and buttons remain live HTML.

## Production assets

- `out/assets/multisport-footer-desktop-56aed11f77ec.webp` — 2172 × 724; 77,844 bytes.
- `out/assets/multisport-footer-mobile-0046bdc50ab7.webp` — 1254 × 1254; 73,338 bytes.

The generated artwork was encoded as WebP for delivery without cropping or compositing. The `<picture>` source changes at 700 px. Narrow phones show the complete square artwork; wider phones and tablets preserve every activity while fitting the footer.

## Desktop prompt

Use case: compositing. Asset: LetsAimIt website footer background illustration. Edit target: the attached existing football-footer-scene.png. Preserve its elegant atmospheric illustrated silhouette style, layered trees, community park setting, muted sage and deep forest green palette, tiny restrained lime accents, and soft seamless fade into solid #143e35 at the bottom. Replace the football-only activity with three clearly readable children’s activities sharing one coherent outdoor community park: a small pair playing football with a recognisable football and a modest goal; a child dribbling a basketball beside a clear basketball hoop/backboard; and two children doing simple fitness/agility exercises with a few cones and low hurdles. Children approximately 7–12 years, natural athletic postures, normal anatomy. Keep the scene uncluttered with generous breathing room. No text, logo, UI, decorative icons, border, collage divisions or watermark. All three activities must be obvious and none should dominate. Format: panoramic 3:1 landscape, about 2400×800. Match the original wide footer composition. Upper 40% is calm uncluttered sky/trees for a white heading and lime CTA to be overlaid by the website. Active children and equipment sit across the lower-middle third, with football toward left, fitness in the middle, basketball toward right; everything inside the central 85% of the image. The last 12% at bottom blends to solid forest green. This is only the background artwork, not a website screenshot.
